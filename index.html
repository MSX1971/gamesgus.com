Innovative mobile games

GamesGus

In GamesGus we want to make unique and innovative games. Influenced by our experience as 8-bit players, the challenge we face is to offer a variety of games that will surprise the current gamer.

Our goal is to entertain and have fun in an authentic way, without dressing in a different way to the same game as always. We believe we have achieved it with New Rue Eur and with our new game Crazy Gumball Machine, and we hope to repeat it with the next releases.

CRAZY GUMBALL MACHINE

New game! Help David separate the gumballs into different flavors. With skill, good reflexes and quick calculations, make the series as long as possible to get the highest score!

NEW RUE EUR

Play with Catalan, Spanish, French and Italian license plates in a single app. Travel through Europe and solve all the traffic jams in their streets!
