New Rue Eur

If you like to gather colors or candies... Try the license plates!

Travel through Europe and solve all the traffic jams in their streets.

In New Rue Eur, you can play with license plates from four countries. In future updates we will travel in more countries!

Select the license plates with equal letters and make them disappear from the screen. You will find cars, vans and trucks, as well as damaged or crashed vehicles, cranes, ambulances and firefighters. Gather these with cars in trouble to solve the queues and get at each level the number of selections requested.

Trucks cause big caravans, so let them go first!

To complicate everything a little more, there are levels with challenges that you will overcome only if you also get a certain amount of vehicles of different colours or measures.

Make the combination as long as you can, you will earn more coins to get help! You will also find levels in which you will have to make calculations with the numbers of the license plate, using all the digits to leave the total to 0.

Get coins to have more time, and especially to use your best partner on this trip... the traffic light!

Its 5 levels of difficulty force completely different strategies of game in each of them, in which the development of the game can give a turn at any moment. A specific license plate can solve the mess you have on the screen, but a truck at the worst time can end your game!
