Crazy Gumball Machine

New game!

Since David always spends all his money on gumballs, his parents bought him a gumball machine for his birthday... which works without coins!

Help David separate the gumballs into the different flavors so he can distribute them among his friends!

With skill, good reflexes, quick calculations and a lot of precision, make the series as long as possible to get the highest score!
